# Source errors and extraction limits

These are historical source extracts, not a validated current procedure manual.

- A fall meeting agenda says cannabis is “NOT PROHIBITED,” conflicting with another meeting agenda. This source wording is preserved, not endorsed. Confirm the current official policy.
- A mini meeting agenda contains the incomplete phone number `314-935-555`. Do not use it as a verified contact number.
- Duty guides have different dates and potentially different handoff expectations. Consult current supervisor guidance.
- OCR can scramble flowchart order and miss text. Consult the preserved image for layout.
- Word text extraction does not guarantee headers, footers, comments, tracked revisions, drawing text, or external linked objects. Images are preserved separately where published.
- Six lockout/key-management documents and their access-control screenshots are withheld from this public repository. Master-key storage locations and the resource-room access code are omitted.
- Staff profiles, financial values, private records, and shared Box capability links are omitted. Follow authenticated source-page pointers.
- Videos, inaccessible Box documents, the linked opening-meeting presentation, external resource sites, and historical calendar entries are not fully extracted.
