## Monthly recognition

The home page says Of the Month (OTM) nominations are due on the third day of each month. Resident Assistant and Social Program are identified as frequently nominated categories. Use the original nomination service for current rules and submission.

## Nomination categories

| Main category | Subcategories shown on the site |
| --- | --- |
| Campus Impact | Institution Faculty/Staff; First Year Student; Student; Organization |
| Housing Impact | Resident Assistant; Student Staff Member; Graduate Assistant; Residence Life Professional Staff; Residential Community |
| Leadership Impact | Executive Board Member; Advisor |
| Program Impact | Educational; Empowerment; Passive; Recognition; Service; Social programs |
