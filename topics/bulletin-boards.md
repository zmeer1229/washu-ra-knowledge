## Working with supplied boards

The source instructs RAs to keep the content of supplied bulletin boards intact and consult their supervisor about permitted theme changes and visual expectations.

## Rubric summary

The rubric uses four performance levels: Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Meets Expectations, and Exceeds Expectations.

| Criterion | Meets Expectations | Exceeds Expectations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Appearance and theme | Coherent, organized layout; backing and borders; tape concealed; mostly clean edges | Creative, polished theme and consistently clean construction |
| Visual appeal | Presentation attracts the reader's attention | Includes a distinctive visual or three-dimensional feature |
| Legibility and space | Readable typed or die-cut information; some space may remain underused | Creative, readable presentation that makes fuller use of the board |
| CEM anchor alignment | Content connects clearly to a CEM anchor | Connection is purposeful, thorough, and impactful |
| Information depth | Informative content with some depth | Substantive material that serves readers with different prior knowledge |
| Interaction | Clear, engaging interactive component | Innovative and especially engaging interaction |
| Spelling and grammar | Few or no errors | Error-free work with close attention to detail |

The lower levels identify missing or inconsistent themes, weak presentation, hard-to-read text, poor spacing, incomplete anchor alignment, shallow information, weak or absent interaction, and language errors. Consult the original rubric for the precise evaluation language.
