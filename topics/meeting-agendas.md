# Seasonal meeting agendas

Source: [Meeting Agendas](https://gowustl.sharepoint.com/sites/WashUResidentAdvisors/SitePages/Meeting-Agendas.aspx)  
Observed: 2026-10-22  
Coverage: paraphrased page guidance and resource references; not the full underlying files.

## Fall arrival and opening

| Meeting | When | Responsibility and purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move-in community meeting | Both first-year move-in nights; possibly transfer arrival night | RAs introduce themselves and explain immediate facilities and policy information |
| Residential community meeting | Sunday morning following first-year move-in | RCD leads, assisted by RAs and faculty; welcomes first-year residents and covers community information |
| Opening and community standards | First Monday of fall classes | Floor/building RAs facilitate community expectations and review university standards |

RCDs create their residential community presentation using the supplied agenda. Locations are communicated during fall training. The source separately links first-year and upper-division opening agendas, a Community Standards and Living Agreement Facilitation Guide, and a fillable standards document. Suite living agreements are in the suites housing portal.

## Winter transition

At the end of fall, floor/building RAs explain how residents should prepare their rooms for winter break, using the transition agenda.

## Spring opening

During the first spring week, floor/building RAs provide semester updates, introduce new residents to the community, and share housing-selection information. The agenda is provided during winter training.

## Spring closing

End-of-semester meetings explain checkout preparation. Follow the current closing agenda and supervisor instructions. This repository does not invent exact meeting dates or requirements absent from the source.
