# Community meeting expectations

Source: [Community Meetings](https://gowustl.sharepoint.com/sites/WashUResidentAdvisors/SitePages/Community-Meetings.aspx)  
Observed: 2026-10-22  
Coverage: paraphrased page guidance and resource references; not the full underlying files.

## Timing and facilitation

RAs are expected to conduct community meetings at the beginning and end of each semester. Coordinate dates and times with supervisors. Facilitation guides provide scripts to support delivery.

## Materials identified on the page

The embedded library lists community standards and living-agreement facilitation guidance, a fillable community standards document, upperclass and first-year floor meeting agendas, a first-year mini floor meeting agenda, and a Fall 2026 opening presentation template. These are indexed by title in the document catalog; their full contents are not included.
