# RA role and communication standards

Source: [About Being an RA](https://gowustl.sharepoint.com/sites/WashUResidentAdvisors/SitePages/About-Being-an-RA.aspx)  
Observed: 2026-10-22  
Coverage: paraphrased page guidance and resource references; not the full underlying files.

## Daily communication

- Review university email and Microsoft Teams at least daily.
- Aim to answer messages and requests within 24 hours on business days.
- Use Teams for resident communication when suitable; use email or in-person follow-up when a matter is more formal or confidential.

## Professional boundaries

Keep communication respectful, inclusive, and professional. Protect confidential student information and internal Residential Life matters. Refer uncertain or sensitive communications to the supervising Residential Community Director (RCD), and promptly report concerns through the established channels.

## Media inquiries

The source says RAs should not represent Residential Life or the university in media statements, including student publications and online outlets. Direct inquiries to the Residential Life Center and notify the supervisor.
