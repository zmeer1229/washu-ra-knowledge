## Departmental account expectations

The source treats community social media accounts as university representation. It says university names, logos, trademarks, images, and campus-building imagery must not imply political or commercial institutional endorsement. It also restricts posts or reposts expressing an explicit or implicit endorsement or opinion.

## Handling negative feedback

The page instructs RAs to capture a screenshot and refer negative feedback to their RCD rather than responding themselves. It says not to delete posts without explicit direction from an RCD or Associate Director (AD).
