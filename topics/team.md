## Mission

Residential Life aims to provide an inclusive, safe environment that supports learning and personal development, while improving services for students and guests.

## Guiding principles

- **Live:** Support safety, comfort, belonging, healthy relationships, empathy, respect, and freedom of thought and expression.
- **Learn:** Connect residential life with intellectual and personal growth; encourage collaboration, faculty relationships, and discussion of diversity and social justice.
- **Lead:** Encourage student participation, responsible citizenship, intentional community design, creative support for student success, and regular evaluation of housing practices.
