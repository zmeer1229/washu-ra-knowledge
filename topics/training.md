The training hub is intended for new and returning RAs. Its presentation link leads to WashU Canvas. Sign in and locate the relevant training material there; no particular Canvas training course or presentation content was extracted.

## Calendar scope

The embedded calendar showed October 18–24, 2026 with zero displayed events. This describes that view only and does not mean the August schedule or the entire calendar is empty. Historical training events and personal attendance information are not copied into this public collection.
