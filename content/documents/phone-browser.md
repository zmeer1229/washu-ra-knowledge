RA Duty Phone Expectations 

Tuesday, January 07, 2025 

4:28 PM 

  

🔁 Duty Phone Turn-in Protocol 

Location: The duty phone should be placed in the appropriate desk drawer in either the RLC or the Village Office no earlier that 8:30am on weekdays and no earlier than 1:30pm on weekends (for the South 40) 

Timely Transfer: There should never be a gap in coverage, if there is no one at the desk when turning in the phone, either wait for someone to get there or notify the RCD on duty 

  

📱 Phone Care and Preparedness 

Keep It Charged: Always return the phone fully charged and ensure it's charged throughout your shift. Place the phone on charge when you return it to the desk as well. 

Volume On, Not Silent: The phone must always have the ringer and vibrate on at an audible level (not on silent or vibrate-only). 

Physically With You: The duty phone must remain on your person or within close reach at all times during your duty shift. Never left unattended or given to a non-RA. 

  

📞 Call and Text Response Expectations 

Immediate Response: Calls must be answered immediately unless you’re responding to an emergency (in which case call back as soon as safely possible). 

Professional Demeanor: Answer with a professional and consistent greeting (e.g., “Hi, this is the RA on duty, how can I help you?”). 

Follow-Up: Record incidents or follow up as needed per protocol. 

  

⛔ Prohibited Uses 

Personal Use Forbidden: No personal texts, calls, social media, or app usage on the duty phone. Even to other current or former RAs. 

No Sharing: Do not lend the duty phone to anyone else, including other RAs not on duty. 

RA Duty Number: The duty phone numbers should NEVER be share with residents 

  

💬 Backup Plan 

If You're Late/Unavailable: Notify RCD on call in advance. It is your responsibility to find a replacement or coverage when needed. 

Phone Malfunctions: Report any issues with the phone (e.g., battery failure, cracked screen, network problems) immediately to the RCD on call.
