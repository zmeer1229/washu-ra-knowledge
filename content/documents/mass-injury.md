## Extracted document text

Mass Injury

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:08 PM

Notify WUPD and RCD On-Call immediately. State exact location, nature of the emergency, and type of help needed. Stay on the line with WUPD dispatch until they okay disconnecting the call. 

Remain with the injured/sick until emergency personnel arrive (as long as it does not put you in harm's way). Then leave if asked to do so to give space and privacy. Apply appropriate first aid assistance only if it is within your training skills and abilities. 

Direct someone to meet the emergency personnel to escort them to the location of the injured people, as long as it does not put anyone in harm's way. Other RAs can also assist with crowd control.

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.  If you don’t have access to Advocate, document the incident in a word document or email and send to the RCD On-Call as soon as possible.
