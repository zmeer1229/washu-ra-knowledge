Bias Incident

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:02 PM

If the bias incident is cannot be connected to a specific person or organization: A bias incident such as this can include but is not limited to letters, posters, flyers, graffiti, telephone calls, and verbal abuse. These are incidents in which there may or may not be a specific individual in the community who is targeted or known.  

If graffiti or physical evidence is involved, do not remove any physical evidence or graffiti until you have documented (photographed). If graffiti has been written, cover the graffiti with paper until it can be permanently removed by facilities staff. If this is a crime, please call WUPD immediately before covering or taking down any material.   

Inform the RCD On-call immediately  

Support and assist the affected residents  

Offer referral to Habif Health and Wellness Mental Health Services   

Offer referral to the Center for Diversity and Inclusion 

Document the incident in Advocate immediately and include all pertinent photos and files  

If the bias incident occurs on social media (ex. Teams): Any bias incident that occurs on social media that may include but is not limited to posting of flyers, jokes, name calling, or bullying language. These may be incidents in which one or more individuals may be targeted.     

Notify RCD On-Call immediately  

Document the incident in Advocate immediately and include all pertinent photos and files.   

Once photos and files are collected and saved, work with Area RCD or RCD on-call to decide when/if item should be deleted (note: this could include a singular message in a thread, or an entire post on within a social media site that the University has control over). 

Work with Area RCD or RCD on-call to decide if any person(s) needs to be removed (temporarily or permanently) from the Teams channel, social media page, etc.  

Work with Area RCD or RCD on-call to draft minimal statement recognizing that an incident occurred and letting students know who to reach out to for in-person follow up while further communication/action is being taken (note: this statement should be no more than 4-5 sentences). Post to the location where the incident occurred.   

Suggested Post: “Hi all. I/we are aware of the social media/Teams post that has raised concerns and has impacted members of our community.  The content has been removed as we are working through this matter.  Feel free to reach out to [RCD NAME]  with questions or concerns.”
