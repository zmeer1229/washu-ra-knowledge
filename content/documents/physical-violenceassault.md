## Extracted document text

Physical Violence/Assault

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:10 PM

Upon receiving a report about an act of violence contact WUPD and RCD On-Call immediately.   

While waiting for WUPD and RCD On-Call to arrive remain with the student and try to identify if medical attention is needed.  

If you arrive at the scene of an active physical assault, facilitate a calm atmosphere in the immediate area, clear out room/area of all students not involved. 

Identify any residents who may have witnessed or have been impacted by the incident.  Move the student(s) away from the scene into a safe area.  Inform the RCD On-Call and record their names for the incident report and for follow-up support.  

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.  If you don’t have access to Advocate, document the incident in a word document or email and send to the RCD On-Call.
