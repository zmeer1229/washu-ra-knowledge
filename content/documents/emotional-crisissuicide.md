Emotional Crisis/Suicide

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:03 PM

Gather all information from the reporting source: 

Name and relationship to student in need of wellness check   

Gather all information related to the student and wellness check: name, age, reason for wellness check (i.e. illness, injury, missing class, haven’t been seen or heard from)   

Notify the RCD of the student in need of the wellness check.  Notify the RCD On-Call if after hours.

If you are already in conversation with the student, follow these steps

Do try to determine what areas are causing concern (academics, finances, relationships, etc.) 

Do express your genuine concern for the resident's wellbeing. You can point to specific behaviors that have caused you or his/her friends to worry.

Do be supportive, sensitive and empathetic. Don't try to psychoanalyze the student. 

Do ask them directly if they are contemplating suicide. Do not be afraid to use the word suicide when talking with the resident.

Do find out if they have a plan and how immediate the plan is. Ask them if they know when, how and where they would do this.

Do let the resident know that people care about them, that you care and that you don't want them to commit suicide. 

Do help the student determine a positive course of action by helping them assess the situation and by identifying other options. 

Do discuss available people and resources that can help. Explain that TIMELYCARE needs to talk to the student via phone. Let them know that the service is free and that many students use it. The RCD needs to be present when the conversation with TimelyCare occurs

Do find out what the resident's plans are for the rest of the evening and the next few days. Try to encourage them to join you for a floor program, meals, etc.

Follow up with your RCD or the RCD On-Call immediately after the interaction. 

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important details.
