Group Disturbances

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:07 PM

Upon receiving a report about a large group disturbance, go to the location to verify the situation.  

After verifying the presence of a large group disturbance, ask if the students present are residents of the community they are occupying.  

Ask all students to present a WashU student ID for documentation, and ask the group to depart from the space.  

Document any additional policy violations, such as alcohol, following standard procedure. 

If students refuse to depart, notify the RCD On-Call immediately and request assistance.   

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.  If you don’t have access to Advocate, document the incident in a word document or email and send to the RCD On-Call.
