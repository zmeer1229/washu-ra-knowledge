Room Condition Reports

Friday, August 09, 2024

1:39 PM

The Room Condition Report (RCR) is a form that students in Residential Life Managed housing complete. It describes the physical condition of the room at move in in August and at move out in May. The room condition report will be completed online via the WashU Housing Portal.

As an RA, you will be going through each space prior to move in to assess the space and place any needed maintenance requests, but will not be marking things on the RCR.

Residents living in the space will also inspect the condition of their room at move in and mark damages on their RCR. The RCR will become available once they are checked in.

Photos: Students can upload photos with their RCRs, however there are restrictions on how many can be uploaded. If they have additional photos to send, they should be sent to reslife@wustl.edu.

Please note: Housing assignments indicating the letter “A” mean the student is assigned to the left side of the room. Housing assignment indicating the letter “B” mean the student has been assigned to the right side of the room. 

Residents should complete this form as they have checked in. It will close early September. 

If students do not complete their RCR at move-in, they will not be able to appeal any damage charges upon move-out.

Accessing the Room Condition Report:

Log into your WashU Housing Portal.

Find the Room Condition Report at the top navigation.

Go to your room and inspect its condition and complete your report.
