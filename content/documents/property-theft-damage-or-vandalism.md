Property Theft, Damage, or Vandalism

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:10 PM

Gather all relevant information of the items stolen, photos of damage/vandalism, and personal information of any resident(s) involved

For situations where resident's items are stolen, damaged, or vandalized, residents can contact WUPD to file a report

If the resident indicates they feel unsafe, notify the RCD on call who can assist with next steps

If the items impacted are community items (not belonging to a specific resident), contact the RCD on call and inform them of the situation

Document the situation in Advocate within 24 hours of becoming aware
