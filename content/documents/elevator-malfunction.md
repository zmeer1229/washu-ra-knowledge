Elevator Malfunction

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:03 PM

If there are individuals stuck in the elevator, find out more information regarding who is in the elevator, how long they have been in there, etc. 

Advise individuals to stay calm and not to try to pry the doors open or exit the elevator. Let the person know you will contact emergency assistance. (WUPD) 

If the elevator is stuck part way between floors or the door is partially open, staff should not attempt to pry the doors to rescue residents. An elevator car stopped between floors could move at any time and is extremely dangerous. Staff should wait for maintenance assistance.  

Contact WUPD and inform them of the situation. If possible, provide WUPD with elevator number or car number.  

Contact the RCD On-Call to inform them of the situation.  Stay with the residents, talk with them and keep them calm until help arrives.  

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.
