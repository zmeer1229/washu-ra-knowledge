Medical Emergency

Wednesday, July 16, 2025

4:50 PM

Resident Advisor On-Call or responding RA: 

If emergency medical care is needed, call WUPD.  WUPD may also notify EST to respond to the emergency call as well. 

Be prepared to describe whether the person is semi or unconscious using the indicators below:  

Alert and oriented. Can clearly communicate person, place and time.  

Verbal limited. Makes limited response to verbal stimuli  

Painful Only.  Will only make limited response to painful/physical stimuli 

Unresponsive. No response to any stimuli  

While waiting for emergency personnel to arrive: 

Facilitate a calm atmosphere, clear out room/area of all students not involved  

Calm students  

Gather personal and SAMPLE information and have available for medical personnel and the RCD On-Call: 

Personal information (if known): 

Person’s name  

Date of birth, current age, class standing (classification)  

Hall room and phone number (and emergency contact if available) 

Parent or guardian’s name, address, and phone number if it is the same as emergency contact.  If not, use the emergency contact if contact needs to be made.  

If transported to a medical facility, which one? Who accompanied the student? 

SAMPLE: 

S -   Symptoms presenting  

A -   Allergies  

M - Medications  

P -   Past Medical History  

L -   Last Oral Intake (time and substance) 

E -   Events Leading to Emergency 

Remain with the injured/sick person until emergency personnel arrives and then leave the immediate area if asked to do so to give space and privacy.  Standby for further assistance until medical personnel have left.  

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.  If you don’t have access to Advocate, document the incident in a word document or email and send to the RCD On-Call.
