WhenToWork - WhenToWork is the scheduling software that the Office of Residential Life uses to organize duty shifts for Resident Advisors. WhenToWork allows RAs (and RCDs) to put in preferences (for when they have a recurring class commitment after 5pm), review published schedules, swap shifts, and see contact information for other employees. WhenToWork has all of the information you need to manage your time for your duty shifts to the fullest—as long as you put in the time to become familiar with it! 

  

Page 1: Getting Logged In to WhenToWork 

oGo to whentowork.com 

oOn the homepage, click the “New Employee?” white text in the upper right-hand corner of the page. 

oOn the next page, scroll down and click “Email my login information” in the lower right-hand corner of the page, under “DON’T know your username/password?” 

oAfter putting in your WUSTL email on the next page, click Send Information. 

oThis will email you a temporary username and password that will look like a jumble of random numbers and letters. Copy the username, then click the blue “SIGN IN” button on WhenToWork. Paste the username, then copy and paste the password below, then click “SIGN IN” again. 

oThe next page is Step 1 – Create Username & Password. You can input any username you’ll remember, and after putting in a password that meets the requirements, you can click the “Step 2” button at the bottom of the page. 

oThe next page is Step 2 – Add/Change Personal Information. You can update your info to include your cell phone (aka the number you can text on) and most up-to-date number otherwise. You can also determine who can view your phone info and email info. WhenToWork has a private messaging system built-in for shift trades, but it is easier if you check All Employees for both, unless your privacy is a concern. 

oYou do not need to put anything in for the address portion, unless you feel so inclined. 

oClick the “Finish” button at the bottom. 

oYou will now be logged into WhenToWork and can sign back out and in with the username and password you just set up! 

Next, you’ll want to do a few steps to make sure that you don’t have messages from WhenToWork marked as spam or junk. 

oIn the Desktop version of Outlook, you can right click on the email in the list, scroll down to the Junk option, then click or hover over this to see the option “Never Block Sender’s Domain (@example.com)” 

o
In the Online version of Outlook, you can select the email in the list, and in the preview window, click the • • • in the top right corner, then click “Add to Safe senders” 

oThe video has examples of what these look like, if you aren’t sure from this description.

Page 2: Inside WhenToWork 

oYou will get an email notification whenever you are assigned to a shift. It will be broken down by week and will let you know what shifts you’re scheduled for that week. If you aren’t scheduled for any shifts that week, it will say that you aren’t scheduled for any shifts. Shifts are displayed in chronological order starting at Monday morning and ending with Sunday evening for a single week. 

oWithin WhenToWork, the “Next Shift” section on the righthand side of the screen shows the immediate next shift that you have coming up, as well as the details for the shift. 

oClicking on “My Schedule,” you can see your schedule in a day-by-day grid, or a graphical view similar to how your class schedule might be viewed. 

oThere are a few specific types of duty shifts RAs are scheduled for: 

For South 40 RAs: 

1:30pm – 5pm desk shift (Sat, Sun) 

5pm – 8:30pm desk shift (T, W, R, F, Sat, Sun) 

5pm – 12am phone shift (no desk – Monday only) 

8:30pm – 12am desk/phone shift with drop off at to the RLC 8:30am the next day (T, W, R) 

8:30pm – 12am desk/phone shift with drop off at 1:30pm the next day (Sat, Sun) 

For Northside RAs, On- or Off-Campus: 

Weeklong shifts beginning on Monday and ending on Monday, carrying the phone at all times 

RAs are only responsible for answering the phone between the the hours of 5PM and 8:30AM 

oYou can also click on “Month” under My Schedule to view it on a month view, or “Upcoming” to view it in a list similar to the email you will receive when assigned shifts. 

oTo view the full schedule of all RAs, click on any of the tabs under “Everyone’s Schedule,” which will show all of the different RA duty positions, and who is scheduled and when. 

And that is how to view your schedule, including seeing what a scheduled shift email looks like, and how to navigate within WhenToWork regarding your schedule. In the next section, we will go over how to trade a shift and any other additional tips and tricks that might be helpful for using WhenToWork. 

Page 3: Trading Shifts in WhenToWork 

To put a shift up for trade: 

oIf you want to give up a shift that you cannot work, click “My Schedule” on the homepage, and then when viewing your schedule, click on a shift that you’re assigned that you want to give up.  

oOnce you’ve clicked on a shift, a pop-up window will appear, which will have the option to “Add shift to trade board.” Click this, and in the next window, you have the option to add any sort of comments for the shift (such as why you are giving it up, for example), and then decide whether you would like to “Trade” the shift, “Drop” the shift, or

2

“Either” trade or drop the shift. Finally, click “Add” at the bottom of the window to add the shift to the trade board. 

oWhatever option you choose will determine how others are able to interact with the shift from their end, which we will go over next. 

To pick up a shift from the trade board: 

oIf you want to see what shifts are posted on the trade board, click “Trade board” on the homepage. You can view the trade board in a few different ways (by week, by month, or by offered shifts), and shifts appear color-coded based on what the poster is looking to do with the shift.  

Shifts that are up for trade will be highlighted in green; shifts that one is looking to drop only and not trade for are highlighted in light blue; shifts that are open to either trading or dropping are highlighted in purple. 

oClicking on a posted “Trade Only” shift will open a pop-up window that will show you what shifts you have to trade in exchange for the posted shift. Clicking on one of your own shifts to trade will open a confirmation window to ensure you want to offer that trade. Clicking “Yes” in this window will send the trade offer to the RA who posted the shift for consideration. Note that this trade is not complete until the RA who posted the shift responds to their message Accepting the shift; they could also Deny the trade offer. 

oClicking on a posted “Drop Only” shift will open a pop-up window that will only give the option to “Click here to request this shift,” since the person who posted it is not looking to trade for another shift, and only wants to drop the shift. Clicking on the option will open a confirmation window to ensure you want to pick up that shift. Clicking “Yes” in the window will confirm the trade pick up and the shift will be officially posted to your schedule and removed from the other RA’s schedule. 

oClicking on a posted “Trade or Drop Okay” shift will open a pop-up window that will show the “Click here to request this shift” option and show you any shifts you have to offer in exchange. You can select from either option in this window, and the confirmation window will reflect the option you choose. 

To review trade offers for a shift you posted: 

oIf you want to see what trade offers you have received for a posted shift, view the lefthand side of the homepage, under “What’s New!” to see any messages, trades offered to you, trades completed, and shifts picked up. Note that unsaved messages are deleted after 30 days. 

Click “# new message(s)” to see a compilation of all messages you have received. 

Click “# trade(s) offered to you” to see what pending trade offers you have received. 

Within this page, you can either “Accept” or “Decline” the offered trade, and clicking “Post” will finalize the shift change and adjust the schedule if accepted, or send the declined offer back to the sender. 

Click “# trade(s) completed” to see the trades you have completed recently. 

Click “# shift(s) picked up” to see any recent shift changes. 

Reviewing the trade board, there are some things to note: 

oSouth 40 RAs can only pick up shifts from other South 40 RAs 

3

oNorthside Off-Campus RAs can only pick up shifts from other Northside Off-Campus RAs 

oNorthside On-Campus RAs can only pick up shifts from other Northside On-Campus RAs 

It is incredibly important that the trade board be updated, even if you arranged for the shift coverage or trade outside of WhenToWork initially, as the RCD On-Call utilizes WhenToWork for the most updated schedule, so WhenToWork must be updated accordingly. 

“Change My Information”: Click this button on the homepage if you need to update your contact information or change your privacy settings between “Only managers can see my phone numbers/email addresses” and “All employees can see my phone numbers/email addresses.” Make sure to click “Save” at the bottom after updating your information. 

“Messaging”: Click this button on the homepage to see the same information as clicking “# shift(s) picked up” under “What’s New!” on the homepage.
