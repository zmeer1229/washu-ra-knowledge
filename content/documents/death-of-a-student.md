## Extracted document text

Death of a Student

Wednesday, July 16, 2025

4:53 PM

Resident Advisor On-Call or responding RA:  

Contact WUPD and the RCD On-Call.  

Do not disturb the scene at all – do not touch the body or items in the room or area.   

Facilitate a calm atmosphere, clear out room/area of all students not involved.  

Calm students.  

Identify any residents who may have witnessed or have been traumatized by the death of the student.  Move the student(s) away from the scene into a safe area.  Inform the RCD On-Call and record their first and last names and student ID for the incident report and for follow-up support.   

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important information.
