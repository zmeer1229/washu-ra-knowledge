Sexual Assault or Misconduct

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:11 PM

Call WUPD immediately, especially if you or the survivor suspects the perpetrator is in the building or if the assault has happened within the past 72 hours. Determine if the person (survivor) has any injuries that require immediate medical attention and notify WUPD that you need medical support. 

Call the RCD On-Call and request immediate assistance with your response. Although the survivor may not want you to call your supervisor, explain that this is a mandatory procedure and that assistance will be provided as privately as possible.  

File an incident report as soon as possible. All known incidents of sexual assault must be reported. Consult with your supervisor or the RCD on duty about how to go about reporting,
