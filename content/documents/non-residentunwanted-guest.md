Non-resident/Unwanted Guest

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:09 PM

If you receive a report of an unauthorized or unknown guest in a residence hall, call the RCD right away to report the situation. 

When reporting the situation, gather any information on if residents have interacted with the individual, where the individual may be at that time, and if they are posing an active threat to the community. DO NOT interact with the individual directly.
