Northside Duty Expectations 

  

Northside RAs 

RA duty shifts on the Northside are for an entire week (Monday to Monday) 

 

RAs on Phone Duty must remain NO MORE than 10 minutes from campus during the entire week that they are On-Call 

 

There are two Duty Phone rotations for Northside RAs 

oNorthside On-Campus - who will respond to calls in Village House, Lopata House, Millbrook Apartments, Village East Apartments, House 1, House 5 and House 7 

 

oNorthside Off-Campus- who will respond to calls in U-Drive Apartments, Greenway Apartments, Loft Apartments, Rosedale Court Apartments 

 

Monday-Friday during the day (8:30am-5:00pm) the Duty Phone can be turned off, but must be turned on at 5PM, sharp 

 

The Duty Phone must be transferred to the next RA on Phone Duty by 7:00pm
