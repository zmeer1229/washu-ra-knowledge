## Extracted document text

Building Evacuation/Fire Alarm

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:02 PM

Immediately evacuate or exit the building and report to the EAP.  

Activate the nearest fire alarm pull station while evacuating the building.  Once outside, notify WUPD then notify the RCD On-Call if they have not yet arrived or been notified.  

Inform WUPD of mobility impaired students needing evacuation assistance, if you are aware of any.  A list of mobility impaired students will be kept electronically in the event of a fire for quick reference.  

Meet WUPD to answer any questions they may have or provide important information regarding students.  Please follow the instructions of WUPD and the Fire Department in the event of a fire evacuation.  

Help direct residents to the appropriate Emergency Assembly Point and do not try to re-enter the building until notified by WUPD and/or the Fire Department.  

Document the incident in Advocate immediately, including all pertinent and important details.
