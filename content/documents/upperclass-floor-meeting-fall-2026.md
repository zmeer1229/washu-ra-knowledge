The goal of bystander intervention programs and medical amnesty programs if for individuals to get the medical attention they need in the moment.  Therefore, you should not concern yourself with how the alcohol or drug incident will affect you/your peer(s) in the long-term, you should be concerned with how NOT seeking medical attention needed in the moment could lead to devastating consequences. / If you are involved in a medical amnesty situation, you will still receive an email from our RCD that will request a meeting about the incident.
