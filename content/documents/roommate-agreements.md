Completing Roommate Agreements

When students move-in, it is important to encourage them to fill out their roommate agreement on the housing portal 

You should highlight these in your first floor meeting and create time for residents to fill these out with their roommates/suitemates 

As the RA, you will not have access to these agreements but you can offer to help your residents fill it out and answer any questions they might have about them 

Updating Roommate Agreements

Roommate agreements cannot automatically be updated in StarRez without administrators re-opening them on the backend. If performing a mediation, residents can take notes individually and review their roommate agreement. If residents would prefer to have their new notes reflected in their roommate agreement, their RCD will need to work with the Operations team to individually open that particular agreement so the residents can update their changes.
