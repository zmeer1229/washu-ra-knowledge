| Part 2: ENGAGE |

| --- |

RA says: Now that you all have completed the community standards process; the living agreement process should be more familiar. 

Living agreements are important for each individual to identify and communicate their own needs and hear the needs of others in order to create a collective understanding of expectations and responsibilities as it relates to a shared living space.

The living agreement will help room/suitemates create strategies for communication, discuss their preferences for sharing a common living space, and decide on mutually agreed upon standards of living.  

In the living agreement template the word “we” is used more often, if not exclusively over “I.”  When you all meet as room/suitemates you should be completing the agreement so that there is mutual agreement on a course of action.  

**Completing living agreements is about negotiating and finding common or middle ground on issues related to living in your space together.**  

Taking the time upon move-in to discuss living and communication preferences will promote a more positive and healthy living experience conducive for studying, sleeping, and thriving.
