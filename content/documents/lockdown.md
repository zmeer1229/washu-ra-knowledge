Lockdown

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:08 PM

Here’s what to expect should a lockdown occur:

Communication

Phone numbers registered in WUSTL alerts will receive a phone call and text message alerting them to intruder on campus

Yellow lights will flash and sirens in buildings will sound (where applicable)

Alert Notification

Multiple alerts will be shared providing updates.

Final message will be an ‘all clear’ 

Lockdown

Card access will allow staff/students swipe in for a short while

After certain time (unspecified), swipe access will be locked and no one can enter buildings

Messaging to Residents

RAs should not send any messages out to residents without the express permission and approval from their RCD or an AD

In most situations, professional staff will be the ones sending out communication to residents
