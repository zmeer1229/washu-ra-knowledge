## Extracted document text

Relationship Violence

Monday, June 9, 2025

2:10 PM

If a resident reports a situation of relationship, follow the same steps you would for Sexual Assault or Misconduct. If the perpetrator is not present or the situation occurred previously, offer support to the student and provide them with resources such as RSVP, Title IX, SARAH, Uncle Joes, CCPS, and your RCD. 

Remember to let the student know that you are a mandatory reporter and document the situation so that relevant offices can reach out.
