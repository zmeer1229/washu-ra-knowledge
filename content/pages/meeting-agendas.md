Orientation and Fall Welcome Agendas
Move-In Community Meetings 

Move-in community meetings occur on the two nights of first-year move-in. The goal of the meetings is to provide a brief opportunity for RAs to introduce themselves and share pertinent facilities and policy information to new residents. Note: If a community has transfer students, they may be required to have a move-in community meeting the night they arrive. The agenda for move-in community meetings can be found at the following link: [Box reference: open the authenticated source page] 

 

Residential Community Meetings 

Residential community meetings occur the Sunday morning following first year move-in and include all first-year residents from the building community. The goal of this meeting is to provide a welcome to all first-year students and share important community information. RCDs will lead the meeting with assistance from their RAs and faculty. Campus locations for these meetings will be provided during Fall training. RCDs will be required to create their own community presentation following the meeting agenda provided at the following link: [Box reference: open the authenticated source page] 

Opening and Community Standards Meetings 

Opening and community standards meetings occur the first Monday of Fall classes, across all communities and are led by the floor or building RAs for that area. During this meeting, RAs will facilitate the creation of community expectations as well as reiterate pre-established standards from the university. Winter Transition Agendas

Winter Transition community meetings occur at the end of the fall semester across all communities and are led by the floor or building RAs for that area. During these meetings, RAs will provide information to students on how they can prepare their space for winter break.

Spring Opening

Spring opening community meetings occur during the first week of the Spring semester across all communities and are led by the floor or building RAs for that area. During these meetings, RAs will provide updates for the Spring semester and introduce themselves and the community to new residents. Additionally, RAs will be responsible for sharing important housing selection information. The spring opening meeting agenda will be provided to RAs during Winter Training.

Spring Closing

Closing community meetings occur at the end of each semester across all communities and are led by the floor or building RAs for that area. During these meetings, RAs will provide information to students on how they can prepare their space for checkout.
