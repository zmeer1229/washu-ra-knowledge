Appearance and Theme

Disorganized or cluttered; no clear theme or visual appeal. Board lacks backing, borders, tape showing, or no clean edges of items.

Theme is present but lacks cohesion; somewhat messy.

Board lacks backing or borders. Tape is showing or few clean edges.

Clear theme with an organized, visually appealing layout.

Board has backing, boarders, no tape showing, and mostly clean edges. 

Theme is highly engaging, creative, and visually polished.

Board has backing, boarders, and no tape showing, all items have clean edges.

Visual Appeal

Information is not presented in an engaging way that grabs the reader's attention.

Most of the information is not presented in an engaging way that grabs the reader’s attention

Information is presented in an engaging way that grabs the reader's attention.

Information is presented in an engaging way that grabs readers attention featuring a signature component (3D/visual piece).

Legibility and Space Usage

Information is not easily legible (i.e. handwritten/drawn not typed or die cut).

 

Items are not spaced out and parts of the board are not utilized/bare.

Some information is not easily legible (i.e. some handwritten/drawn not typed or die cut).

 

Some information is not spaced out or the board appears crowded.

Information is easy to be read (I.e. typed or die cut). 

 

Space on the board is used but still possesses negative/underutilized space.  

 Information is easy to be read (I.e. typed or die cut) and presented in a creative way. 

 

The space on the board is maximized with little/no negative space. 

Content Alignment to Anchors.

Content is irrelevant or does not align with a CEM anchor. 

 

Content partially aligns with a CEM anchor. 

 

Content aligns well with a CEM anchor. 

 

Content is fully aligned with a CEM anchor, purposeful, and impactful.

Depth of Information

No depth of information presented.

Content lacks depth and features a limited amount of information

Content features some depth and is informative.

Content has depth and is substantive for people at various points of learning about the topic.

Interactive Element

No interactive element included.

Interactive element is minimal or unclear.

Includes a clear and engaging interactive element.

Interactive element is innovative and highly engaging.

Spelling and Grammar

Frequent spelling and grammar errors.

Some spelling and grammar errors.

Few to no spelling and grammar errors.

Error-free and demonstrates attention to detail.
