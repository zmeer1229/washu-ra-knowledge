Welcome from Associate Director, Dr. Domonique Crosby

Hi RAs,

 

First things first, thank you!

 

Thank you for saying yes to this role. Thank you for your willingness to lead, to support your peers, and to be a part of something bigger than yourself. The Resident Advisor position is one of the most impactful student leadership roles on campus, and we do not take your commitment lightly.

 

Whether this is your first year as an RA or you are returning with some experience under your belt, know that your presence matters. You help shape the residential experience for hundreds of students. You are often the first person they turn to with questions, with concerns, and in moments of celebration or challenge. That is no small thing.

 

This manual is here to help you along the way. It is a guide to the policies, practices, and resources you will need to feel grounded and confident in your role. It will not have every answer, but it will point you in the right direction. Keep it close and do not be afraid to ask questions as you go.

 

I want you to hear this clearly: we see you, and we appreciate you!! Your time, energy, compassion, and care are what make our communities feel like home. It is not always easy, but it is deeply meaningful, and you are not doing it alone. You have a team behind you that believes in you and is here to support you every step of the way.

 

Best,

Dr. Domonique Crosby

Associate Director for Residential Education

Mission

We seek to provide a safe environment that encourages learning and personal growth in an inclusive community that empowers and challenges our residents. We continually strive to improve our services to meet the needs of our students and guests and reflect the high standards of WashU. 

Guiding Principles: 

Live: Provide a safe, clean, comfortable, secure and supportive community. Offer opportunities for students to develop a sense of belonging to the campus and residential community. Guide students in developing healthy social relationships. Promote mutual concern, empathy and respect. Practice the moral and legal rights to free thought, speech and opinion.

Learn: Cultivate an environment that facilitates personal and intellectual growth and development. Create a seamless living and learning environment. Engage in discussions grounded in issues of diversity and social justice. Foster collaborative learning among students, staff and faculty. Encourage students to develop meaningful relationships with faculty.

Lead: Engage in opportunities for student involvement and leadership. Ensure that intentionality is the driving force behind the creation of the physical environment of our residential communities. Develop creative initiatives to promote student success. Evaluate existing practices on a regular basis to remain a leader in all aspects of university housing. Promote an environment where staff role model our commonly held principles in the conduct of our duties. Encourage and expect students to act as responsible citizens.
