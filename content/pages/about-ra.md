About Being an RA
This is the page for job descriptions, expectations, communication standards, work agreement, evaluation material, and accountability information. 

Communication Standards
Monitor Communication Platforms Daily 

Check Microsoft Teams and university email accounts at least once per day to stay up to date on important information from supervisors and the Residential Life department.

Respond to messages and requests in a timely manner, ideally within 24 hours on business days.

Media and Public Statements 

RAs must not speak to the media (including student publications, local news outlets, or online platforms) as a representative of the Residential Life department or the university.

If contacted by media, RAs should refer all inquiries to the Residential Life Center (reslife@wustl.edu) and let their supervisor know of the request. 

 

Communication with Residents 

Use Microsoft Teams as the primary method of communication with residents, when appropriate.

Maintain professional tone and boundaries in all written and verbal communication.

When necessary, follow up in person or via email for more formal or confidential matters.

Professional Conduct and Confidentiality 

All communication, whether digital or in person, should reflect respect, inclusion, and professionalism.

Do not share confidential student information or internal Residential Life matters outside of authorized channels.

Escalation and Reporting 

If unsure about how to handle a communication (e.g., media request, sensitive resident issue), consult your supervising Residential Community Director before responding.

Report any concerning communications or incidents promptly through the proper channels.
